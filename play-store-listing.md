## Full Description

**Tasting Party** is a personal tasting journal for food and drink products. Create tastings, add products by scanning a barcode, searching a product database, or entering them manually, rate each product on the scale you choose, and keep your own notes.

**Key features:**
- **Barcode scanning** — point your camera at a product and add it instantly
- **Product search** — find products by name from a public database
- **Manual entry** — add any product with name and brand
- **Flexible rating scales** — rate every product with 5 stars, 10 stars, or a 0-100 slider
- **Personal notes** — write your own tasting notes per product
- **Voice notes** — dictate your tasting notes with the microphone and have them transcribed on-device, in English or Spanish
- **Tasting sessions** — group products into named tastings with descriptions
- **Share a tasting** — send a tasting to a friend as a link or a file; ratings are never shared, and the recipient adds it only after a preview confirmation
- **All products view** — see every product across all your tastings, sorted by most recent
- **Backup & restore** — export all your tastings as a JSON backup and import it back anytime, or move it to another device
- **Spreadsheet export** — get a CSV view of your tastings and ratings for Excel or Google Sheets
- **Bilingual** — available in English and Spanish
- **100% offline** — all your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

Whether you're tasting wines, cheeses, coffees, craft beers, or anything else, Tasting Party keeps your personal reviews organized and private.
