## Full Description

**Tasting Party** is a personal tasting journal for food and drink products. Create tastings, add products by scanning a barcode, searching a product database, or entering them manually, rate each product on the scale you choose — 5 stars, 10 stars, or a 0–100 slider — and keep personal notes, including voice notes transcribed on your device. Product details also show ingredients, calories, and nutrition fields so you can compare what you have tried.

**Key features:**
- **Barcode scanning** — point your camera at a product and add it instantly
- **Product search** — find products by name from a public database
- **Manual entry** — add any product with name and brand
- **Flexible rating scales** — rate every product with 5 stars, 10 stars, or a 0–100 slider. Changing scale never rewrites saved ratings, and unrated products stay clearly unrated
- **Personal notes** — write your own tasting notes per product
- **Voice notes** — dictate your tasting notes with the microphone and have them transcribed on-device, in English or Spanish
- **Tasting sessions** — group products into named tastings with descriptions
- **Share a tasting** — send a tasting to a friend as a link or a file, with notes or names only. Ratings are never shared, and the recipient sees a preview before adding it
- **Nutrition details** — see ingredients, calories, proteins, sugars, saturated fat, fibre, sodium, and alcohol for each product; empty fields are hidden
- **All products view** — see every product across all your tastings, sorted by most recent
- **Daily testing reminder** — during closed testing, get one local reminder a day for 14 days; nothing is sent to a server
- **Backup & restore** — export all your tastings as a JSON backup and import it back anytime, or move it to another device
- **Spreadsheet export** — get a CSV view of your tastings and ratings for Excel or Google Sheets
- **Bilingual** — available in English and Spanish
- **100% offline** — all your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

Whether you're tasting wines, cheeses, coffees, craft beers, or anything else, Tasting Party keeps your personal reviews organized and private.
