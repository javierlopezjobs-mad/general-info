## Full Description

**Tasting Party** is a personal tasting journal for food and drink products. Create tastings, add products by scanning a barcode, searching a product database, or entering them manually, rate each product from 1 to 5 stars, and keep your own notes.

**Key features:**
- **Barcode scanning** — point your camera at a product and add it instantly
- **Product search** — find products by name from a public database
- **Manual entry** — add any product with name and brand
- **Star ratings** — rate every product from 1 to 5
- **Personal notes** — write your own tasting notes per product
- **Tasting sessions** — group products into named tastings with descriptions
- **All products view** — see every product across all your tastings, sorted by most recent
- **Bilingual** — available in English and Spanish
- **100% offline** — all your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

Whether you're tasting wines, cheeses, coffees, craft beers, or anything else, Tasting Party keeps your personal reviews organized and private.
